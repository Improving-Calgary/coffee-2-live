# AI for Developer Productivity - Coffee Stories

 ## Searching and Filtering 

As a new customer,  
the number of coffees available is very high.  
Help me filter and sort on the page so I can search for the coffee I am looking for 

## Coffee Profile 

Each coffee should have a custom profile page when clicked on. People should be able to leave comments about their experience with the coffee. 

## Coffee Upvoting 

The default sorting order should be by coffee popularity on the home page. An upvote button appears by each coffee to track up votes. Users can vote however many times they want to show their dedication. 

## Taste Profile Onboarding (harder, more thought needed)

As a new customer,  
I want to take a quick taste quiz and optionally import past coffee purchases so the AI can build my profile,  
so that my sampler is immediately tailored to my preferences.  
Give me a quiz that uses the properties of coffee and ask me friendly questions and give a top 3 recommendations.
